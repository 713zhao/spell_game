## Full description (max 4000)
AI Spell Game helps children learn to spell. They hear a word, spell it, and get instant, friendly feedback while building vocabulary and confidence.

SPELLING PRACTICE
• Listen and spell – every word is read aloud with text-to-speech.
• Your school's lists – practise weekly spelling lists or add your own words (parents can add words and labels).
• Review what's tricky – the app brings back words that need more practice.
• Progress tracking – scores, streaks and levels show how a child is improving.
• Chinese support – pinyin and character practice.

STAY MOTIVATED
• Daily goals build a learning habit.
• Earn coins and gems and unlock fun rewards in the Game Store, including short mini games such as Merge Ten and Color by Number.

MADE FOR FAMILIES
No ads. No in-app purchases. Optional parent email is used only to help recover a forgotten password. Parents can delete their child's account and data at any time.
